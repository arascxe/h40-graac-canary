# MDRTF — ATTENTION ISSUANCE SHADOW V1

Frozen: 2026-09-19 UTC

Status: future-only research; `PAPER_ONLY / CAPITAL_LOCKED`. This lane cannot
deploy a contract, create a coin, sign a transaction, spend funds, publish a
promotion, or change AFT/PPSC admission.

## Objective

Test one issuer-side hypothesis without contaminating the trading experiment:

`NEW EXTERNAL ATTENTION -> INDEPENDENT QUORUM -> CONTINUOUS PUMP CENSUS -> NO FUTURE TOKENIZATION -> DUE-DILIGENCE CANDIDATE`

The highest automatic output is `LAUNCH_DUE_DILIGENCE_CANDIDATE`. It is not a
recommendation or authorization to launch.

## Frozen admission rules

- The shadow clock begins with an empty candidate ledger. Earlier attention
  rows may veto novelty but may never be promoted retrospectively.
- The same normalized attention event must persist in at least two future-only
  cuts.
- It must have at least two geographies or two named independent publishers.
- A title observed before the shadow start, or separated by at least 24 hours
  within the preceding seven days, fails the provisional low-base novelty gate.
- Candidate age may not exceed six hours from first shadow observation.
- Every Pump census cut from attention observation through evaluation must be
  `COMPLETE_INTERVAL`. A baseline, gap, 429, parse error, or missing cut is
  insufficient coverage, never evidence that no token exists.
- Any conservative semantic match against a future-only Pump launch name or
  symbol vetoes tokenization whitespace.

## Claims deliberately not made

`FUTURE_PUMP_WHITESPACE_ONLY` means only that no semantic launch was observed
in the covered future-only Pump census known through evaluation. It does not
prove:

- absence before the future-only census;
- absence on another launchpad or chain;
- trademark, copyright, publicity, or other naming rights;
- official affiliation with the attention subject;
- organic buyer demand, executable liquidity, or expected profit.

Therefore canonicality beyond the measured window and rights remain
`UNVERIFIED`, and `actionable_launches` is always empty in V1.

## Explicit exclusions

- no hidden or related-wallet simulation;
- no wash trading, fake volume, fake affiliation, or outcome-based naming;
- no creator allocation, sell schedule, launch transaction, or wallet secret;
- no historical winner tuning or backfilled candidates;
- no conversion of missing data into PASS;
- no automatic alerts unless a future version is separately admitted.
