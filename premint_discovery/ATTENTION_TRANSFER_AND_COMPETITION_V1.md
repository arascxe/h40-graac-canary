# FEE100K: Attention Transfer and Launch Competition (research spec v1)
Date: 2026-09-23
Status: DESIGN ONLY. This document changes no production scoring thresholds, cron, trigger, or deployment.
Constraint: ZERO_COST_ONLY; no paid boosts, KOLs, fabricated traction, impersonation, coordinated wash trading or artificial buyer inducement.

## The hypothesis to falsify
A financially relevant pre-mint external attention object must not merely go viral.
It must transfer *voluntary* attention from independently participating communities
into sufficiently many interested crypto-native observers and buyers to sustain
genuine creator-fee-bearing turnover on the *correct* canonical pool.

Proposed causal stages:
external birth -> independent transformed replication -> exact coherent object
-> demonstrably vacant token leader -> public crypto-native crossover
-> original legible packaging + transparent fee ownership
-> authentic launch discovery -> organic participation
-> graduation (optional milestone, NOT primary success criterion)
-> retained independent turnover -> verified creator-vault receipts.

## Real competitive landscape
- Social/trend systems: TikTok Creative Center, Brandwatch, Google Trends, etc.
  They overlap with attention discovery; proprietary systems may already do more
  than their public product descriptions reveal. Do not claim we are first.
- On-chain terminals: GMGN, Axiom, DEX Screener, bots. They lead in post-mint
  liquidity/maker/holder/price discovery; our research MUST focus on pre-mint
  object-level evidence rather than competing in latency after mints exist.
- Launchpads: Pump.fun, Bags and others supply curve/liquidity/fee primitives.
  They are infrastructure; every other creator using them competes for attention.
- Direct rivals: independent original creators, fast deployers/copycats and
  token communities that already have distribution. First mint is NOT a moat.

## Core unit of competition
ONE specific observable internet object, NOT a vague meta/hashtag or profile.
Freeze all potentially equivalent visual/text/name/token variants as one
family BEFORE selecting a launch candidate. Record existing competing mints,
their ages, provenance, actual organic holder/maker evidence, and the
degree to which the public already associates one with that object.
If an established authentic token already dominates: abstain. Never present
an unofficial derivative as endorsed/official/first.

## Proposed research-only new features (do NOT backfill with post-event leakage)
1. cross_crypto_independent_references: count of *publicly observable* independent
   crypto-native discussion of the external object BEFORE our mint.
   Do not include our own campaign posts, related-wallet accounts, sponsored
   mentions or bot-style syndication.
2. identity_transformation_proof: adjudicated different-actor transformation
   supported by real visual/semantic evidence, not hashtag use or re-posting.
3. exact_object_copycat_saturation: point-in-time family-level on-chain
   occupancy including current recognizable canonical leader.
4. packaging_legibility: blind human assessment of 32-pixel icon,
   2-second recognition, one-line explanation, shareable original format.
   This is a research proxy, never a calibrated probability.
5. distribution_readiness: actual ability to publish a coherent *original*
   asset pack to relevant voluntary audiences before/at mint. Follower count
   and paid promotion are not prerequisites for a pilot; empty channels are
   explicitly recorded as poor distribution.
6. fee_receipt_integrity: chosen launchpad, exact issuer/fee-owner wallet,
   routeable canonical pool, effective fee schedule, documented sharing.
   Verify via a minimal authorized real trade only after approval.

## Three gates; preserve existing frozen Demand-Proof threshold
Gate A OBJECT: real multi-actor transformed replication, identity coherence,
independent sources, existing Demand-Proof frozen rules; semantic vacancy
confirmed for variants, not only exact canonical URL.
Gate B TRANSFER: evidence the object has organic relevance in actual crypto
communities, rather than millions of views from non-trading audiences;
independence of observed interest from any of our own launch marketing.
Gate C EXECUTION: a complete original 32px-identifiable icon, short looping
asset, name/ticker and one-line concept that accurately depicts the object;
attribution/rights review; transparent unofficial status and creator-fee
disclosure; verified issuer and fee routing; competing leader check.
ABSTAIN if any hard gate fails. Do not lower frozen Gate A for volume.

## Organic launch packaging: no paid-plan dependency
Prepare three rights-safe transformative artwork/text packages for a single
already-external object. Evaluate blind by recognition and clarity using
available genuine feedback, not synthetic likes. Publish context-rich,
source-attributed, clearly issuer-associated content only where
self-promotion is allowed and people can voluntarily follow.
Separate observations of pre-existing external replication from responses
to our own content. A GitHub Pages public provenance/contract page may be
used if available on the existing free plan; do not fake official affiliation.
At mint, synchronize the truthful source-to-asset story, contract address,
fee-recipient disclosure, and risk statement across available channels.
Do not advertise price guarantees, false scarcity, fabricated buyers or
undisclosed paid/related-account endorsements.

## Funnel and economic ledger
Measure separately:
T-1 independent external object mentions/actors/surfaces/remixes;
T-1 independent crypto-native crossovers and timing;
T0 competing mints + recognized leader status;
T0/T+15m/T+60m/T+24h actual distribution impressions/clicks *where genuinely
measurable*, genuine unique makers, wash/bot exclusion, canonical turnover,
curve progress/graduation, organic non-creator holding, creator wallet
receipts and 24h follow-on turnover.
Distinguish marketing attributed traffic from external independent data.
Do not infer buys or conversion from raw views; no manufactured volume.

Fee objective: verified NET revenue from actual canonical qualifying trades
to our controlled creator vault, not nominal volume multiplied by platform
TOTAL fee. Use realized effective rate by market-cap band and owner share;
record alternate routes with no creator fee. Include launch/claim/gas costs.
At 0.30% owner fee $100k requires ~$33.3m qualifying turnover; at 0.50%
~$20m; at 0.95% ~$10.5m; these are illustrative effective-rate scenarios,
not forecasts or a claim the higher rate applies to every trade.

## First no-money experiment
Prospectively archive 20 specific object candidates including matched
negative objects from the same surfaces/time windows. Blindly record at
pre-mint T0 whether independent non-crypto virality crosses into independent
crypto-native conversation and whether a genuinely vacant canonical object
exists. For high-confidence cases prepare original packaging; run small
authentic, disclosed, no-paid-media posts if allowed and record actual
engagement separately from classifier evidence. Compare to equally active
objects that failed crossover. No live mint until a manually approved
pilot meets integrity gates. Stop if there is no repeatable crossover or
if packages repeatedly lack voluntary organic distribution.

## Critical constraints
- A post-mint trending screen is not pre-mint alpha; it can inform postlaunch
  distribution diagnostics, not selection at T-1.
- Graduation is not $100k; the real endpoint is independent canonical
  turnover and actual fee recipient receipts.
- A viral object may be owned/licensed or contain trademark/likeness rights.
  Do not claim endorsement or reuse protected assets without appropriate
  rights; make original work where possible.
- Promotional/legal obligations depend on audience/jurisdiction; disclose
  creator interest and any compensated promotion, follow platform rules.
- Keep production code / data / immutable first-freeze intact and zero-cost.
